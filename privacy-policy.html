Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Academic Edge handles information when you use the Academic Edge application and related services.

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. About Academic Edge

Academic Edge is a student-focused application designed to help users manage academic information such as attendance, timetable, study materials, academic dates, productivity information, and related student tools.

For privacy questions or requests, users can contact us through the Feedback section of the Academic Edge website.

2. Information We May Handle

The information handled by Academic Edge depends on the features you choose to use. This may include:

Account and profile information

  • Name or display name.
  • Academic profile information that you choose to provide, such as semester, course, branch, batch, or related details.
  • Authentication information where an account-based feature is enabled.

Academic information

  • Attendance records and attendance-related information entered or generated within the app.
  • Timetable and class information.
  • Academic calendar information and events.
  • Subjects, notes, assignments, study materials, bookmarks, downloads, and other academic content you choose to store or manage.

Documents and files

If you choose to upload, import, save, or process a document, the app may access the file and information contained in it to provide the requested feature. We do not claim ownership of your academic documents.

AI interactions

If you use Aeri AI or another AI-powered feature, the text, questions, or other information you submit may be processed to generate a response. Only information necessary to provide the requested AI feature should be submitted.

Important: Academic Edge should not be used to store passwords, payment-card information, government identity numbers, or other highly sensitive information unless a specific feature explicitly requires it and the app clearly explains why.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate Academic Edge features.
  • Save and display your academic information and preferences.
  • Calculate or display attendance and academic insights.
  • Provide timetable, calendar, notes, document, and productivity features.
  • Respond to questions and requests submitted through feedback.
  • Provide AI-powered functionality when you choose to use it.
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

Academic Edge does not intend to sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate particular Academic Edge features. Depending on the feature and the current app implementation, these may include infrastructure, authentication, database, analytics, hosting, or AI service providers.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies. We aim to use only services that are necessary for the relevant feature and to limit information shared with them to what is needed to provide that feature.

5. Firebase and Cloud Services

Academic Edge may use Firebase or other cloud infrastructure for features such as authentication, database storage, application services, or related functionality. Information stored through these services is handled according to the configuration of the app and the applicable service provider's terms and privacy practices.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Data transmitted between the app and supported online services may be protected using standard encrypted network connections such as HTTPS/TLS where supported.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should also protect their account credentials and avoid sharing them with others.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant feature, maintain the service, meet legitimate operational needs, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and the feature involved.

8. Data Deletion and Account Deletion

If Academic Edge provides account creation, users will be provided with a way to request deletion of their account and associated personal data. Requests may also be initiated through the Academic Edge website's feedback/contact mechanism.

Where deletion is requested, associated information will be deleted or de-identified as required by applicable requirements, except where retention is necessary for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other permitted purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

Academic Edge is intended for general student use and is not specifically directed to children under the age applicable to children's privacy laws. We do not knowingly request or collect children's personal information for purposes that are not permitted by applicable law.

10. Your Responsibilities

You should only upload or submit information that you have the right to use and that is necessary for the feature you are using. Please review documents and information before sharing them with any AI or cloud-based feature.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Academic Edge adds, removes, or changes features or when applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

12. Contact and Privacy Questions

If you have a privacy question, data request, or concern about Academic Edge, please use the Feedback / Contact section on the Academic Edge website and clearly mention that your request is privacy-related.

13. Agreement

By using Academic Edge, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where applicable law requires consent for particular data processing, the app will provide the relevant consent or permission flow.
