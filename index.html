Is Academic Edge free to use? +
Yes! Academic Edge offers a robust free tier that includes your basic timetable, attendance tracking, and document storage. Premium features like advanced AI assistance are available on our student-friendly Pro plan.
Can I import my university schedule automatically? +
Absolutely. You can upload your PDF timetable or use our automated calendar import tools to populate your weekly schedule within seconds.
How does the Aeri AI assistant work? +
Aeri reads through your uploaded lecture notes and textbooks to provide contextual answers, summarize dense paragraphs, and help you study efficiently without leaving the app.
Is my student data secure and private? +
We take your privacy seriously. All documents and personal academic data are encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell or share your information.
